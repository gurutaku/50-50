🧠 Fairness check
A fair coin gives each side the same chance on every toss: 1 out of 2, or 50%.

Three things to remember 🌟

1. Each toss is independent

The last toss does not control the next one. After 5 Heads in a row, Tails is still a 50% chance on the next toss.

2. Small samples can look lopsided

10 tosses might look like 7–3. That does not prove the coin is unfair.

3. More tosses reveal the pattern

With lots of tosses, the Heads and Tails percentages tend to get closer to 50–50.
